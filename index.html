LevKoll

A personal order tracker for Android.

LevKoll keeps track of things you have ordered online. It reads order confirmations and shipping notices in your Gmail and shows, in one list, what is on its way, what is waiting at a pickup point, and what has arrived.

It is a private, non-commercial project. It is not published in any app store and is used only by its developer and family.
